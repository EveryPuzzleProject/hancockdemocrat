# A numbered crossword series in The Hancock Democrat, 1924–25

The Hancock Democrat, a Thursday weekly of Greenfield, Indiana, began printing a numbered,
titled crossword on December 4, 1924: "CROSS-WORD PUZZLE No. 1", "STEPPING STONES", under a
standing paragraph "HOW TO SOLVE A CROSS-WORD PUZZLE", with "(Copyright, 1924.)" beneath the
grid and "The solution will appear in next issue." This is the Every Puzzle Project's record
of the series as this paper printed it. Part of the
[Every Puzzle Project](https://github.com/EveryPuzzleProject).

The puzzles were not made in Greenfield. The numbering, the titles, the standing how-to and
the copyright line with no owner named are the marks of a feature sold to many small papers.
The Democrat is where it was found, so the record starts here; if the series' owner is
identified, the record should be named for the series and this paper becomes one of its copies.

## What's here

`puzzles.tsv` has one row per page saved: date, page, the number and title as printed, the
copyright line, and notes. Six pages so far, Nos. 1 to 6 (1924-12-04 to 1925-01-08). The
numbers and titles were read by OCR for this index and checked against the page for Nos. 1
and 5 only.

## Whose series it is

Probably the Western Newspaper Union's; not proven. No syndicate is named in the Democrat, but:

- The Norfolk Journal and Guide printed "STEPPING STONES" (1925-01-31) and "THE FOUR ANGLES"
  (1925-02-07), the titles of Nos. 1 and 3 here, with the same "(Copyright, 1924.)" line
  ([journalandguide](https://github.com/EveryPuzzleProject/journalandguide)).
- The Pittsburgh Courier's puzzles in the same type, from 1925-01-24, carry
  "(© by Western Newspaper Union.)"
  ([pittsburghcourier](https://github.com/EveryPuzzleProject/pittsburghcourier)).

**Grids compared, 2026-11-19** (black-square patterns read by xword-ocr's grid finder):

- No. 3 here and the Journal and Guide's "THE FOUR ANGLES" are the same grid (4 of 169
  squares read differently, which is reading noise).
- No. 1 here and the Journal and Guide's "STEPPING STONES" agree in the lower half and
  differ in 11 of 121 squares, all in the upper half, where this paper's scan is faint.
  Probably the same grid; not proven by this reading.
- None of the Courier's Western Newspaper Union grids (its Nos. 2 and 4 to 9, 1925-01-24 to
  03-14) matches Nos. 1 to 4 or 6 here, and none of the Afro-American's six copyrighted
  grids (1925-02-21 to 03-28) does either. That neither confirms nor rules out one series:
  if the papers ran it in step, those weeks would be this series' Nos. 8 to 17, and only
  Nos. 1 to 6 are saved here. (No. 5 here was not found by the grid finder.)

So the Democrat and the Journal and Guide carried the same series. That it is the Western
Newspaper Union's rests on type and wording alone until the Democrat's pages for
1925-01-15 to 03-28 are compared, or a page shows a title and the syndicate's name together.

## What isn't known

- **Proof of the owner**: a copy with both a title from this series and a syndicate line.
- **How long it ran**, here or anywhere. Only the first six weeks are saved.
- **Where the solutions are.** Each puzzle promises its solution "in next issue"; the pages
  saved are the puzzle pages, and the solutions have not been looked for.
- **Whether other papers' puzzles are the same series.** The Afro-American of Baltimore
  printed six puzzles in the same style with "(Copyright, 1925.)" from 1925-02-21
  ([afroamerican](https://github.com/EveryPuzzleProject/afroamerican)); they have not been
  compared with this series.

## Status (2026-11-19)

Six sample pages saved and indexed. Nothing has been read by the OCR pipeline. Clues and
answers are not typed in here.
